How FixRoute Works

Four clear, actionable steps from the moment something goes wrong to official resolution.

1

Describe Your Problem

Type what went wrong in your own words. FixRoute identifies the core issue and jurisdiction without requiring bureaucratic knowledge.

2

Identify the Authority

Discover the exact regulatory body, ombudsman, or grievance officer legally responsible, backed by verified Indian statutes.

3

Prepare & Draft

Follow an interactive evidence checklist, generate a polite and formal complaint draft, and download it as text or print to PDF.

4

Track & Escalate

Log your complaint reference number, set follow-up milestones, and view pre-vetted statutory escalation options if deadlines lapse.

Our Commitment to Accuracy & Privacy

FixRoute operates on eight core principles to eliminate the confusion citizens face when resolving everyday grievances in India:

🔒 Zero Data Harvesting

We never ask for sensitive credentials such as Aadhaar OTPs, banking PINs, or CVVs. All complaint drafts and records are private to your session.

📜 Verified Statutory Sources

Every route cites real government acts, gazette notifications, or regulatory guidelines (RBI, TRAI, UGC, Consumer Protection Act 2019). We never invent contact numbers.

🛡️ Independent & Transparent

FixRoute is an independent civic utility founded by Sreekar to empower consumers. We clearly indicate verified versus user-submitted information.
