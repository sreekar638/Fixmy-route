Frequently Asked Questions

Does FixRoute submit complaints on my behalf?

No. FixRoute is an independent guidance and drafting tool. By law in India, complaints must be submitted by the consumer themselves to ensure official standing and security. We provide the verified official portal link and pre-drafted legal letter so you can submit in minutes.

What is an Ombudsman?

An Ombudsman is an independent official appointed by a statutory regulatory body (like the Reserve Bank of India or Electricity Regulatory Commissions) to investigate complaints against companies. Their services are free for citizens.

Can I use FixRoute without creating an account?

Yes! You can search all problem directories, view resolution steps, complete evidence checklists, generate formal letters, and track complaints in guest mode. Creating an account simply lets you access your data across devices.

How is FixRoute funded?

FixRoute was founded by Sreekar as an independent public utility project. We do not sell user data, take commissions from disputed entities, or run deceptive ads.
