Founder & Project Vision

Fixing the Dispute Maze in India

Every day, millions of people in India face unresolved consumer issues, erroneous bills, digital payment failures, or tenancy disputes. Finding the right door to knock on shouldn't be harder than the problem itself.

Message from the Founder

"I founded FixRoute with a simple conviction: when an everyday problem strikes — whether an unexpected ₹10,000 electricity bill, a failed UPI debit, or a withheld college caution deposit — citizens shouldn't have to wander through broken government portals or give up in despair.

FixRoute is designed not as a static list of links, but as an actionable, step-by-step problem navigator. It tells you exactly who holds legal accountability, what evidence you need in hand, and how to draft a professional complaint that gets results."

— Sreekar
Founder & Architect, FixRoute

Our 8 Product Principles

1. Simplicity Above All

Users should understand what to do without needing legal expertise or bureaucratic jargon.

2. Strict Factual Accuracy

We never invent authorities, fake helplines, or fabricate legal claims.

3. Radical Transparency

Every recommended route explains why it was selected and cites real official gazettes, RBI circulars, or ministry rules.

4. Absolute Actionability

Every page gives users a tangible next step, an evidence checklist, or an editable letter draft.

5. Privacy by Design

We collect zero financial secrets, passwords, or Aadhaar OTPs. Data stays private to the citizen.

6. Universal Accessibility

Responsive layouts that work seamlessly across mobile smartphones and desktops.

7. Trust & Verification

We clearly differentiate human-verified routes from community suggestions or AI hints.

8. Nationwide Scalability

Engineered to expand from national dispute directories to state and municipal levels across India.
